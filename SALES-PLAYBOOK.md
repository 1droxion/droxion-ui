# Droxion — First 3 paying customers playbook

## Sell an outcome, not AI
Offer: "We help HVAC shops respond to missed and after-hours calls so they can capture service requests and book appointments." Never promise a guaranteed revenue increase.

## Narrow pilot (first 14 days)
- Target 50-100 independently owned HVAC repair firms in Tupelo and nearby Mississippi markets with a published business phone and website, avoiding unsolicited messaging where consent is required.
- Ask the owner whether calls are missed after hours or while technicians are on jobs, and whether they use a booking calendar.
- Offer a live, clearly labeled AI demo using the business's publicly available business-hours/service-area facts, with no real customer data or routing before permission.
- Record each contact in a sales CRM: company, channel, date, reply, pains, next follow-up and permission.
- Aim for 10 genuine discovery conversations, 3 demos, and 1-3 paid pilots; targets, not forecasts.

## Short pitch (call or in-person)
"Hi, I'm Dhruv with Droxion. We help HVAC businesses answer overflow and after-hours calls with a virtual receptionist, capturing the customer's details and booking available appointments. Are missed calls a problem for your team? I can show a short real demonstration if it's relevant."

## Ask in discovery
- How many inbound calls and after-hours calls do you get weekly?
- What happens when staff can't answer?
- What is one booked job worth on average? Avoid exaggerating.
- What calendar/software do you use?
- Who handles emergencies and unusual calls?
- Would a paid pilot with a defined trial period and clear limits be useful?

## Suggested paid offering (confirm with each client)
- Managed AI receptionist: $499/month for 300 included voice minutes.
- Setup: $500 one time.
- Quote exact overage price in writing **before** collecting payment.
- Define support hours, cancellation terms, privacy, consent, acceptable use, emergency routing and limits in the signed agreement.
- Payment only after the caller handoff/booking flows pass tests.

## Track success
Weekly: companies contacted, qualified conversations, demos completed, paid customers, average call-answer rate, real bookings, errors, cancellations, refunds and voice-minute costs.
Do not count simulated calls as customer bookings.
